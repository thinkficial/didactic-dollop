A few things, made clear

What happens when I prepare a message?+

Your details are added to a draft in your chosen email app or WhatsApp. Review the draft and send it there. This website does not send the message for you.

Can I ask about training?+

Absolutely. Tell us the skills you’re interested in and what you’d like to achieve. You can also explore TEN Academy before getting in touch. Contact TEN to confirm current courses and availability.

What should I include in a project enquiry?+

Share what you want to build or improve, who it is for, and any timing or requirements you already have in mind. It’s fine if you’re still working out the details.
